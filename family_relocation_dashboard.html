Executive summary

The model prioritizes career market, school quality, affordability, rental feasibility, tax burden, university proximity, and Gregg-specific AI/robotics lifestyle fit.

Core conclusion

The strongest overall shortlist is Round Rock / Cedar Park, Cary, Madison / Huntsville, Suwanee / Peachtree Corners, and Oviedo. These locations balance AI/product career options, strong family infrastructure, long-term housing discipline, robotics or engineering adjacency, and proximity to universities that support Gregg’s future teaching ambitions.

The strategic answer is Texas. The best research-and-teaching answer is Cary. The best value robotics answer is Madison / Huntsville. The best Georgia family-access answer is Suwanee / Peachtree Corners. The best Florida no-income-tax answer is Oviedo.
